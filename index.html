Process overview
  1. Customer provides master copy in brief template or in Word or PPT.
  2. Reviewed by REPH and submitted into XTM for translation.
  3. REPH creates project in XTM, assigns reviewers (designated Field Marketer or Cactus). Project is run, Field Marketer reviews, recommends edits, notes specific phrasing to be added to glossary for that language (e.g. alternatives to "research-grade"). Edits made, final review, approval. Approved copy becomes the locked source in XTM.
  4. REPH sends completed translation document(s) to the requestor.
  5. Translated content uploaded by REPH to the appropriate areas (Frontify, Contentful, etc.)
  6. Refer to the Elsevier Brand Style Guide for local language guidance on brand voice and style, along with a multilingual term base.
